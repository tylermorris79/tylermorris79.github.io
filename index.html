Hearth Hub

A private family calendar display.

Hearth Hub is a personal project. It reads its owner's own Google Calendar events and shows them on a wall-mounted screen in his home, alongside local weather and a view of the town square.

It is not a product, it is not offered to anyone else, and it has exactly one user.

What it does

  • Reads events from calendars the owner's own Google account can already see
  • Shows the times and the day's shape on a screen at home
  • Nothing is published, shared, sold or sent anywhere else

Access it uses

Read-only. The single permission requested is calendar.events.readonly. It cannot create, change or delete anything on any calendar.
