Privacy Policy

Hearth Hub · last updated 25 October 2026

Hearth Hub is a personal, single-user application. It is operated by its owner for his own household and is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, Hearth Hub requests one Google permission, calendar.events.readonly. This is read-only. The application cannot create, modify or delete any calendar or event.

It reads only from a fixed list of calendars written into its own configuration. It does not browse or enumerate other calendars.

What it stores

A short-lived copy of upcoming events, covering about seven days ahead, is cached so the display does not have to query Google continuously. The cache holds start and end times, whether an event lasts all day, and which calendar it came from. By default, event titles are replaced with the word "Busy" before anything is stored.

The cache is overwritten on every refresh and expires automatically.

Where it goes

To one screen, in the owner's home, on his own network. Nothing is shared with, sold to, or transmitted to any third party. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no other user.

Credentials

The Google credential is held server-side as an encrypted secret. It is never included in any web page, never sent to the display, and never written to logs.

Retention and deletion

Cached events are transient and are replaced or expired automatically. Access can be withdrawn at any time by the owner at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately and permanently ends all access. No copy of the data survives in any other system.

Limited Use

Hearth Hub's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the address listed as the developer contact on the application's Google consent screen.
